      Welcome, Commander! 🌕

      You command a Moon outpost with a crew of 4. Keep everyone alive and the base running for 10 days.

      1. Check the Base — watch the meters.
      2. Choose Your Plan — food, oxygen, mode and resupply.
      3. End the Day — resources are used up.
      4. Respond to Events — solar flares raise radiation. Use Shelter to stay safe.
      5. Learn and Continue — every day teaches you something new.

      Careful: supplies are delayed, and fixing one problem can cause another. There is no single perfect plan!
